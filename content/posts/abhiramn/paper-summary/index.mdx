# First Draft Summary: TruthfulRAG

Below is my initial draft for the paper summary assignment.

## What are the authors proposing?
They propose TruthfulRAG, a RAG system that uses knowledge graphs and entropy-based conflict detection to resolve disagreements between internal model knowledge and retrieved facts.

## Motivation
RAG systems often fail when the model’s internal knowledge conflicts with updated or more accurate retrieved information. Prior methods don’t capture factual structure well enough to resolve these conflicts.

## Approach
TruthfulRAG builds a knowledge graph from retrieved text, retrieves relevant reasoning paths, and uses entropy changes to determine which paths fix model misconceptions.

## Results
TruthfulRAG outperforms standard RAG, KRE, COIECD, and FaithfulRAG across several datasets, especially on multi-hop and time-sensitive tasks.

## Is the comparison fair?
Yes. They evaluate multiple models, use a shared retrieval setup, and include ablations.

## Authors’ takeaways
Structured reasoning paths reduce uncertainty and improve factual correctness in RAG.

## My takeaways
The method is simple, practical, and fits neatly into existing RAG systems.

## Would I use this?
Yes — especially for medical, financial, legal, or fast-changing domains.

## Remaining problems
Triple extraction errors, scaling KG creation, entropy threshold tuning, and handling conflicts between multiple external sources.
