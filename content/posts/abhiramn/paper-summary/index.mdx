# First Draft Summary: TruthfulRAG

## Paper Summary Proposal

**Citation**  
Liu, S., Shang, Y., & Zhang, X. “TruthfulRAG: Resolving Factual-level Conflicts in Retrieval-Augmented Generation with Knowledge Graphs.” AAAI 2026.  
PDF: :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

**Brief Description**  
This paper introduces TruthfulRAG, a Retrieval-Augmented Generation framework that uses knowledge graphs to identify and resolve factual conflicts between an LLM’s internal knowledge and retrieved external documents. Instead of operating at token or semantic level like previous approaches, TruthfulRAG extracts factual triples, constructs reasoning paths, and uses entropy-based filtering to ensure the generated answers remain consistent with reliable external evidence.

**Motivation**  
I selected this paper because it addresses a critical failure mode in modern RAG systems: contradictions between retrieved facts and outdated model memory. The authors propose a structured and fine-grained approach using knowledge graphs and uncertainty analysis, which aligns with my interests in LLM reliability, RAG pipelines, and graph-based reasoning. Additionally, this is a recent and less widely summarized paper, making it suitable for the assignment.
