## Description

_In your own words, what is this paper about?_

The authors propose a method of identifying ambiguous words through
an unsupervised machine learning process.
Their method uses CBOW embeddings (need to confirm)
and something called DBSCAN
(Density-Based Spatial Clustering for Applications with Noise)
to filter out common contexts (noise) between what are otherwise clusters
in the distributions of like words; these multiple clusters thus represent the
multiple meanings of ambiguous words, identified by the process itself
(by constrast, they point out that other approaches to ambiguity have relied
on supervised/manual identification of candidate words).
They highlight its usefulness for identifying ambiguous words' meanings when
taken in isolation (need to review what the claim here is).

#### What are the authors proposing?

##### Prompt

```markdown
What are the authors proposing?
```

The authors are proposing an automated method to identify and resolve word ambiguity using the following key components:

1. **Word Embeddings**: They use the CBOW architecture from the word2vec model to generate word embeddings from a large corpus of German Wikipedia articles.

2. **Context Vectors**: For each occurrence of a potentially ambiguous word in the corpus, they generate context vectors by summing the embeddings of nearby words (5 to the left and 5 to the right).

3. **DBSCAN Clustering**: They apply the DBSCAN clustering algorithm to the context vectors to identify clusters corresponding to different meanings of a given word.

4. **Automatic Parameter Selection**: They propose an automatic method to select optimal DBSCAN parameters (epsilon and minPts) to maximize the quality of clusters. This is done by evaluating the clustering quality using a parameter score that considers the number of clusters, average silhouette score, noise ratio, and other metrics.

5. **Cluster Labeling**: They describe two methods for labeling the clusters, one based on similarity to other words in the input space and another based on the predicted center word from the word embedding model.

6. **Ambiguity Resolution**: By identifying clusters corresponding to different meanings of a word, the method aims to resolve ambiguity by providing context-specific labels for each cluster.

Overall, the authors are proposing an unsupervised, automated approach to identify and resolve word ambiguity using a combination of word embeddings, clustering, and parameter selection.
