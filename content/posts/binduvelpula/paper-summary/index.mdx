## Description

This paper introduces a hybrid authorship-verification method by combining a text-to-text transformer (T5-large) with stylometric features. The transformer is used to capture semantic context by extracting its last hidden state, and the stylometric features include part-of-speech tags, punctuation usage, emojis, and topic/author-specific statistics. The authors train a model on pairs of texts to decide whether the same author wrote them.

## Motivation

I selected this paper because it aligns directly with my hybrid stylometry + transformer project. The authors use a T5 transformer plus stylistic features, which is very similar to my plan of combining sentence embeddings with traditional stylometric features.
